# In this town, everyone has a number above their head

One line = one shot. `(silent)` lines are beats with no narration.

1. In this town,
2. everyone has a number above their head.
3. It counts your friends.
4. Make a friend, it goes up.
5. Lose one, it goes down.
6. People read your number before your face.
7. High numbers get the best seats.
8. Low numbers eat alone.
9. The mayor has four hundred and twelve.
10. His daughter Ivy has eighty-seven.
11. More than any kid in town.
12. And then there's Sam.
13. Sam's number is zero.
14. It has been zero his whole life.
15. Every morning, he says hello to everyone.
16. Nobody says it back.
17. They look up.
18. They see the zero.
19. And they keep walking.
20. So Sam does his kind things at night,
21. when nobody can see who did them.
22. Then he lies in bed, and looks up.
23. Still zero.
24. But one night, a storm rolled in,
25. and Ivy took a dare.
26. Climb the tower. Touch the bell.
27. Eighty-seven friends cheered her on.
28. Then the lightning hit.
29. And Ivy was hanging from the ledge by one hand.
30. She screamed for help.
31. Eighty-seven friends looked up.
32. And every single one took a step back.
33. Her number started to fall.
34. Sixty.
35. Thirty.
36. Nine.
37. Zero.
38. (silent beat: empty)
39. Only one kid ran toward the tower.
40. The kid with no friends at all.
41. Two hundred and twelve steps.
42. He didn't stop once.
43. He grabbed her wrist,
44. and pulled with everything he had.
45. (silent beat: tumble)
46. Ivy looked at the space above his head.
47. Still zero.
48. Then she looked at her own.
49. One.
50. By morning, the whole town had changed.
51. The mayor's four hundred and twelve?
52. Three.
53. Because the number never counted the friends you have.
54. It counts the ones who would come for you.
55. And Sam's was still zero.
56. Because nobody had ever come for Sam.
57. Until Ivy sat down next to him.
58. And for the first time in his life,
59. Sam's number moved.
60. It didn't go up.
61. It went to minus one.
62. Somewhere above the town,
63. someone had just started counting too.
64. (silent beat: end)
